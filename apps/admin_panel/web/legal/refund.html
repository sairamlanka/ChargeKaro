Cancellation and refund policy

Version 0.1 (draft) · Last updated 4 November 2026

Draft for testing. This is placeholder text and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be replaced before ChargeKaro launches.

The booking fee

The booking fee (currently ₹12) reserves a charging point for your chosen time. It is non-refundable, including when you cancel or do not reach the station in time.

When you get your money back

  • Payment taken but booking not confirmed: if money leaves your account but the booking is not confirmed (for example, the slot was lost while the payment was slow), it is refunded automatically.
  • The station cancels: if the station cancels your booking, the fee is refunded.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Cancelling and moving a booking

  • You can cancel up to 60 minutes before the start. The fee is not refunded.
  • Instead of cancelling, you can move the booking to another time once, before the cut-off, and keep the fee you paid.

Charging costs

Charging is paid to the station at its own rates. Questions about charging bills go to the station first. Use Help in the app if you need us.
