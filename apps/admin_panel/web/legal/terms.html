1. What ChargeKaro does

ChargeKaro lets you find electric vehicle charging stations and book a time slot at a charging point. Stations are run by independent businesses ("station owners"). ChargeKaro does not run the stations or supply electricity.

2. Your account

  • You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code. Keep your phone secure; bookings made from your account are your responsibility.
  • Give correct details. You can book for another driver by adding their name and mobile number to the booking.

3. Bookings and the booking fee

  • A booking reserves a charging point for the time you choose. It is confirmed only after the booking fee is paid.
  • The booking fee (currently ₹12) pays for the reservation. It is not a charge for electricity. Charging is paid to the station at the station's own rates.
  • Arrive on time. Check-in opens a few minutes before the start, and the point is held only for a short time after the start. After that the booking may be marked as a no-show.
  • You can move a booking to another time once, before the cancellation cut-off, without paying the fee again.

4. Refunds

See the refund policy.

5. Using stations

Follow the station's rules and staff instructions. Station owners are responsible for their equipment, prices, opening hours and safety. Report problems with a station from the app.

6. Our responsibility

We try to keep slot and charger information correct, but stations can change availability, close or have faults. Where a station cancels your booking, the booking fee is refunded. To the extent the law allows, ChargeKaro is not liable for losses caused by a station, a vehicle or a charger.
