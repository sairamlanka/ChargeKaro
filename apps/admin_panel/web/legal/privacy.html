Privacy policy

Version 0.1 (draft) · Last updated 4 November 2026

Draft for testing. This is placeholder text and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be replaced before ChargeKaro launches.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, mobile number and, if you add one, an email address for receipts.
  • Bookings: the stations, times and payments of your bookings, and the driver's name and number if you book for someone else.
  • Location: your location only while you search for nearby stations or plan a route. It is not tracked in the background.
  • Device: a notification token if you allow notifications, and basic crash reports to fix bugs.
  • Vehicle: battery size and range you enter for estimates, kept on your phone.

How we use it

  • To make and manage bookings, and to send confirmations and reminders by SMS, email or notification.
  • To share your name, booking reference and time with the station you booked, so its staff can check you in.
  • To process payments through our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not store your card or UPI details.
  • To fix problems and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

The station you book, and service providers that help us run ChargeKaro: hosting (Supabase), payments (Razorpay), messages and notifications (SMS and email providers, Firebase). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.

Your choices

  • Update your details, turn off reminders or notifications, or delete your account in Profile in the app.
  • Booking and payment records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account.
